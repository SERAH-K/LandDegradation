Our Points System

We believe every tree counts in our fight against climate change. That's why we reward your efforts with our points system.

For every 100 trees you plant and verify through our platform, you earn 10 points. The more trees you plant, the higher you climb on our leaderboard!

Points Calculation

100 trees planted = 10 points

Example: If you plant 350 trees, you earn 35 points
